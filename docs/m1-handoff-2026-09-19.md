## 1. The objective

**Get M1 working: beat v16 with a decimal-week peak estimate.** Not reproduce
v16 — v16 is the floor, not the target.

### Why v16 is only a floor

v16's peak estimate is **integer-valued**. Against decimal truth it cannot
score better than its rounding residual. Decomposing v16's 0.640 wk:

| | seasons | mean err |
|---|---:|---:|
| v16 gets the **right week** | 6 of 10 | **0.077** wk (pure rounding) |
| v16 **misses the week** | 4 of 10 | **1.485** wk |

Headroom, measured:

| scenario | result | gain |
|---|---:|---:|
| perfect decimals, same weeks as v16 | 0.594 wk | 0.046 |
| fix the 4 missed weeks, stay integer | 0.046 wk | **0.594** |
| both | 0.000 wk | 0.640 |

**93% of the available gain is week-level accuracy on four seasons
(2017-18, 2018-19, 2019-20, 2022-23); 7% is decimal precision.** Decimal week
is *necessary* — it sets the floor at 0.077 instead of 0 — but the work is in
the four seasons v16 gets wrong by 1–2 weeks.

Current code already emits decimal peaks. It is worse than integer v16 anyway
(2.731), so decimal output alone buys nothing without stability.

### Targets

| gate | target | current | v16 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| mean abs final peak error, 10 shared seasons | **≤ 0.40 wk** | 2.731 | 0.635 |
| seasons where estimate misses the week (≥0.5) | **≤ 2 of 10** | 9 | 4 |
| un-latch events | **≤ 1** | 34 (8/11 seasons) | 1 |
| forecast MAE | **no worse than 9.253 pp** | 9.253 | — |
| impossible forecasts (>40% positivity) | **0** | 49 of 635 | present in v16 too |

0.40 wk requires beating v16 outright: right week in ≥8 of 10 seasons *plus*
working decimals. It is deliberately below v16's 0.635 and well above the
0.046 ideal.

Weighting stays **50/50 peak vs forecast** (settled earlier). Detection at or
after the peak is worthless; earlier strictly beats later.
